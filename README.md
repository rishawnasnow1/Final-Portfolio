# Final-Portfolio
Sprint and Retrospective project demonstrating Agile and Scrim practices. 

How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?
I start by breaking down some of the needs the user may have. I focus on what their problem might be and try to solve them. Some of the stories I created helped me  organize the needs because I am able to describe features from the user perspective. That way it is easier to understand what needs to be buold to ensure the users expectations are being met. 

How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
I start by developing programs step by step and testing while I plan. I used Agile practices such as breaking work into smaller task, working into sprints and continously improving based on feedback. I will continue using Scrum practices like Sprint planning, daily stand-ups and restrospectives to remain efficient while developing. 

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
Being a good team member means to communicate clear and allow feedback, while also support members on the team. It also means being  responsible for your work while also contributing to the teams overall success. Collaborations is important but so is accountability and effieciency.
